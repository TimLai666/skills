# Learning Depth and Practice

Read before content analysis, concept writing or practice generation in any mode.

## Depth and coverage

- Maintain a complete topic checklist mapping each topic to its treatment and source. Do not silently omit topics to shorten the vault.
- Core concepts explain mechanisms, assumptions, applicability, exceptions, common confusions and necessary worked examples. The learner should be able to explain why and apply the idea to a new situation.
- Supply prerequisite knowledge needed for that understanding even if the source mentions it briefly. Distinguish verified supplementary material from source claims and cite its basis; mark unresolved gaps.
- Supporting topics get enough detail to explain their relationship to the core. Group related material when that improves understanding; do not force every passing term into a separate textbook-length note.
- Preserve all members of classifications and their meaningful differences. Tables and summaries support explanation rather than replace it.
- In codebase mode, ground explanations and exercises in actual code paths, interfaces, failure behavior and dependencies.

## Practice

- Document and Wiki modes: create practice for every learning topic, covering its important concepts. Start with about eight questions per topic and a mix of roughly 50% recall, 25% application and 25% analysis. Adjust count and mix to coverage and learning goals, not arithmetic quotas.
- Codebase mode: start with five exercises per major module, covering code reading, configuration, debugging and extension where relevant; adjust to actual module responsibilities.
- Each practice set includes application and analysis, not just recall. Analysis should require comparison, reasoning about conditions, diagnosing misconceptions or explaining an unfamiliar case.
- Verify that important mechanisms, exceptions and confusable concepts are tested. Add questions when coverage is missing; removing repetitive questions must not remove unique learning coverage.
- Keep answers folded, with sufficient reasoning to teach the solution. Do not truncate explanations just to meet a line count.
- Record why a material adjustment to depth or practice coverage fits the learning goal, and report remaining source or coverage gaps.
