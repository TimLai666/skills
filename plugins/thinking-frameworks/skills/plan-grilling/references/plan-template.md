# Plan and decision templates

Update the existing plan when one exists. Use the sections needed to retain
the substantive analysis and decisions; do not create empty log entries.

## Decision record

```markdown
### [Decision topic]
- Decision: [what was agreed]
- Reason: [evidence and tradeoff]
- Important alternative: [if relevant, why it was not chosen]
```

## Plan

```markdown
# Plan: [what is being planned]

## Problem
[The pain, who encounters it, evidence and why action is needed]

## Reframe
[If useful: the clarified problem and why it changes the approach]

## Scope decisions
### Chosen scope
- [Verifiable outcome or activity] - [reason]

### Explicitly out
- [Item] - [reason]

### Deferred
- [Item] - [reason or condition for revisiting]

## Assumptions to validate
- [Assumption] - [consequence if wrong and how to check it]

## Success criteria
[Observable result, method of assessment and relevant timeframe]

## Failure modes
- [Risk] - [mitigation]

## Decisions
[Substantive choices and important tradeoffs]

## Open decisions
[Unresolved choices that affect proceeding, if any]

## Next step
[The next action and the actual document path needed for handoff]
```
